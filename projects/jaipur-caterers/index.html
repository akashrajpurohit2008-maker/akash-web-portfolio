Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum guest count? +
Our minimum is 30 guests for home gatherings and 50 guests for all other event types. For very large events (500+), please contact us at least 3 weeks in advance.
Do you provide serving staff and equipment? +
Yes — all our packages include trained serving staff, buffet counters, chafing dishes, and crockery. For Gold and Platinum packages, we also include table linen and decoration for the buffet area.
How far in advance should I book? +
We recommend booking at least 2–3 weeks in advance for smaller events and 4–6 weeks for weddings. However, we do accommodate last-minute requests based on availability.
Do you offer free tasting sessions? +
Yes! For confirmed bookings above ₹50,000, we invite you to our kitchen for a complimentary tasting session so you can approve the menu before your event.
Are you 100% vegetarian? +
Yes — Jaipur Caterers is a 100% pure vegetarian catering service. We do not serve or prepare any non-vegetarian food. All our menus, kitchens, and utensils are strictly vegetarian.
Can you accommodate Jain or special dietary needs? +
Absolutely. We offer Jain, satvik, diabetic-friendly, and allergen-aware menu options. Simply mention your requirements when enquiring and we'll build the menu accordingly.
What areas in Pune do you serve? +
We serve all areas across Pune and PCMC — including Kothrud, Baner, Hinjewadi, Wakad, Viman Nagar, Hadapsar, Kondhwa, Pimpri-Chinchwad, and more. For outstation events, contact us to discuss logistics.
